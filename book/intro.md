# Legal Network Analysis

This open textbook introduces network analysis applied in a legal context. It is intended for readers with no, or only limited, familiarity with network analysis. The book introduces and explains concepts in a concise manner, illustrated with examples. No prior experience with network analysis is assumed, and no technical background is required.

The textbook is freely available. You can simply download the PDF and read it as a conventional book. For many readers, this will be the most straightforward way to engage with the material. At the same time, the book is fully open-source. All content, including text, data, and code, is available in a public repository. This allows for different modes of use, depending on your interests and level of experience:

- You may read the material passively through the PDF.
- You may run the accompanying Jupyter notebooks to reproduce figures and results, or to explore the examples interactively.
- You may adapt the material to your own needs by creating your own version of the repository (commonly referred to as “forking” the repository, i.e. making a personal copy that you can modify independently).
- You may contribute to the book by suggesting changes, correcting errors, or adding new sections or chapters. Contributions of this kind may, where appropriate, be acknowledged through authorship or contributor status.

In this way, the book is not only a static introduction, but also a living resource that can evolve over time. We hope that this combination of accessibility, transparency, and openness makes the material useful for a wide range of readers.

How to use this book:
- Read chapters in order via the left-hand table of contents.
- Open notebooks to explore code and datasets directly.
- Refer to the Appendices for hands-on setup and utilities.

The code-part of the textbook is done exclusively in [NetworkX](https://github.com/networkx/networkx). Faster libraries are available for those who need them, but this remains for the reader to explore.

Happy reading!
